Ice Cubes from Scratch

Materials and Ingredients

  • Ice Cube Tray
  • Freezer
  • Purfied Water

Steps

  1. Ensure your ice cube tray is clean and ready for usage.
  2. Fill each section within the ice cube tray evenly with purified water
  3. Carefully place the tray filled with water into the freezer to allow to cool.
  4. After several hours of freezing, your tray full of ice cubes made directly from scratch should be finished
