Making a piece of toast

Materials and Ingredients

  • Toaster
  • Butter knife
  • Sliced Bread
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Put a slice of bread within the toaster.
  2. Set toaster to desired amount of time.
  3. Depress toaster lever and wait.
  4. When the toaster finishes the toast, remove the bread.
  5. Butter the bread using the butter knife.
  6. Enjoy your toast
